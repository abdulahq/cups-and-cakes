The Craft

A home kitchen
that takes its time

Cups and Cakes began in a single home kitchen on Street 39 — one oven, one piping bag, and a stubborn belief that baking deserves patience. Every sponge is weighed by hand. Every swirl is piped slowly. Every custom cake is a conversation first, a dessert second.

There is no assembly line here. Just small batches, real ingredients, and a quiet standard that has earned the trust of our regulars across Islamabad.
